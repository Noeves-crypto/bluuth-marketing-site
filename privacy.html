3. Who processes data for us

We use a small number of providers (processors) to run Bluuth:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts this website. It may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.
  • Render (Render Services, Inc., USA) hosts the client portal and booking system at portal.bluuth.com.
  • Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc.) may handle traffic to portal.bluuth.com for security and performance.
  • mailbox.org (Heinlein Support GmbH, Germany) handles our email.

Where a provider is based outside the EU/EEA, the transfer relies on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

4. How long we keep it

  • Enquiries and booking details that don't lead to work: up to 12 months after last contact.
  • Client project data and portal accounts: for the duration of the work and up to 12 months after, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Invoices and bookkeeping records: 7 years, as required by Swedish law (bokföringslagen).

5. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to processing, and receive it in a portable format. Email kevin.d@bluuth.com and you'll get an answer within one month.

6. Complaints

If you think we handle your data wrongly, tell us first. You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection, Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY), www.imy.se.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date.
